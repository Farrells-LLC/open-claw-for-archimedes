## Manual Upload: TikTok

1. Open TikTok.
2. Tap `+`.
3. Choose `Upload`.
4. Select the four images in this order:
   - `01_data_organization.png`
   - `02_reports.png`
   - `03_predictive_evaluations.png`
   - `04_combined_workflow.png`
5. Use photo mode/slideshow.
6. Paste Caption 4 from `ARCHIMEDES_TIKTOK_IG_DRAFT_PACK.md` for the first combined test, or use each matching caption if posting separately.
7. Save as draft or post manually.
